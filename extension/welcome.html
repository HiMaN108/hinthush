Welcome to HintHush

Cleaner "click & hold" pages, and a quick way to test your own quiz before it goes live.

What it does

🏷️ Hides "Click & hold" labels

Works on every website. Hint text like "Click & hold to reveal" disappears, while pictures, diagrams and answer options stay exactly where they are.

👁️ Reveals blurred content, for your own pages

On pages running on your computer (localhost, 127.0.0.1, *.localhost, *.test), blurred questions are shown all at once so you can check your quiz layout quickly.

Get started in 3 steps

Pin the extensionClick the puzzle-piece icon 🧩 in Chrome's toolbar, then the pin next to HintHush, so its icon is always visible.
Let it run on websitesGo to chrome://extensions → HintHush → Details → Site access and choose On all sites. If you skip this, the popup shows an Allow on this site button you can click per site.
Open a page and click the iconThe popup shows how many labels were hidden and areas revealed on that page. The number on the icon shows the same count. Use the switches to turn features on or off, or Pause on this site to leave a site untouched.

Questions

Why is "Reveal blurred content" greyed out on most websites?

Revealing is meant for testing your own quiz pages while you build them, so it only runs on pages served from your own computer. Live websites are left exactly as their owners designed them; only the hint labels are hidden there.

The popup says "Chrome is blocking HintHush on this site"

Chrome's site access for the extension is set to "On click". Press Allow on this site in the popup, or set site access to "On all sites" (step 2 above).

A label isn't being hidden

Open Settings and add the label's exact wording under "Label phrases to hide". Labels drawn as images, or added only through CSS, can't be detected.

I changed something and nothing happened

Switches apply instantly. If you edited the extension's files, click the reload ↻ icon on HintHush in chrome://extensions, then refresh the page.
